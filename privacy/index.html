Privacy Policy

Whose Pic? Party Game · Effective October 25, 2026

Whose Pic? is a party game: everyone's phone picks a few random photos from their own library, and the group guesses whose camera roll each one came from. This policy explains what the app collects, who can see it, and how long it is kept. In short: no account, no ads, no tracking, and your photos are deleted shortly after the game.

Whose Pic? is made by Shahreare Khan, an independent developer. Questions or requests: diurnal.spurge6c@icloud.com.

Your photos

  • When you join a game, the app asks for access to your photo library and picks random photos for you to review on your phone. You can reshuffle them as often as you like before anyone sees anything.
  • Nothing is uploaded until you tap Lock in. Then only the items you kept (up to 16) are uploaded.
  • Before upload, photos are resized and re-encoded, which removes location (GPS) and other embedded metadata. The month and year the photo was taken are kept and shown to the other players when the photo is revealed.
  • Your uploads are shown only to the players in your game. They are not public, not sold, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI.
  • The practice game uses built-in sample photos and never touches your library.

Services we use

  • Supabase hosts the game backend: database, file storage and the anonymous sign-in. Your uploads, name, player ID and game data live there while they are kept (see below).
  • RevenueCat manages the in-app purchase. It receives your player ID, purchase details, and technical details such as device model, iOS version, app version, language, App Store country and an app-specific device identifier (IDFV). RevenueCat's privacy policy.
  • Apple processes payments and offer codes. We never see your payment details.
  • Lorem Picsum (picsum.photos) serves the random stranger photos in wildcard rounds. Your phone downloads them directly, so that service sees your IP address. No personal data is sent to it.

Like any internet service, these providers receive your IP address when the app connects to them.

How long things are kept

  • Photos and game data are deleted automatically within about two hours after a game ends. Unfinished games are deleted after a few hours without activity, and nothing is kept longer than about a day. The host can also tap End & delete everyone's photos to delete them right away.
  • If you leave or are removed during a game, your photos stop being shown to anyone right away. The files are deleted then, or at the latest with the rest of the game.
  • The app clears other players' photos from your phone's image cache when a game ends or you leave.
  • Your random player ID, your unlock status and the purchase record are kept so your unlock keeps working. Players you block stay blocked until you tap Unblock everyone in Settings or ask us to delete your data. Reports are kept for up to 90 days.

Your choices

  • The practice game works without photo access. Real games need it so the app can pick random photos; you can change it at any time in iOS Settings, including limiting it to selected photos.
  • To have your player ID, unlock record and any remaining data deleted, email diurnal.spurge6c@icloud.com with your Player ID (in the app: Settings → Your Player ID). We'll confirm within 30 days. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your phone.

Safety

Games are private rooms of up to 12 players who join with a code, and everyone agrees to the Terms of use before hosting or joining. Anyone can report a photo or a player from inside the game, anyone can block a player (we keep a record of who blocked whom so it keeps working), and the host can remove a player, who then can't rejoin. We review reports within 24 hours.

Children

Whose Pic? is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we'll delete their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
