How we enforce it

  • Report: any player can report a photo or a player from inside the game. A reported photo is hidden on the reporter's phone straight away.
  • Block: any player can block another. You won't see their photos or reactions, and neither of you can join a game the other is in.
  • Remove: the host can remove a player, who can't rejoin that game; their photos stop being shown.
  • We review reports within 24 hours and act on them, which can include removing content and blocking a player from the game for good.
