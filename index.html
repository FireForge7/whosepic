How to play

  1. One person taps Host a game and shares the 4-letter code. Everyone else taps Join and types it in. You need at least two phones.
  2. Each phone picks random photos from its own library. Look them over and reshuffle as often as you like, then tap Lock in.
  3. Photos pop up one at a time. Guess whose camera roll each one came from. Faster correct guesses score more.

No friends around? Tap Try a practice game on the home screen to play against three computer players with sample photos.

Questions

Who can see my photos?

Only the players in your game, and only the ones you locked in. Location and other hidden metadata are removed before upload, and everything is deleted shortly after the game. Details are in the privacy policy.

Someone joined who shouldn't be there, or posted something offensive

The host can remove a player from the lobby or during the game; removed players can't rejoin and their photos stop showing. Anyone can tap Report on a photo or a player, and anyone can Block a player (tap ⋯ next to their name): you won't see their photos or reactions, and neither of you can join a game the other is in. A reported photo is hidden on your phone right away, and we review reports within 24 hours. For anything urgent, email us. The house rules are in the Terms of use.

What does the $2.99 unlock include?

It's a one-time purchase, not a subscription. Every game you host gets 20 and 30 round games, wildcard photos, blur reveal, double points, streak bonuses, and single-photo swaps. Only the host needs it; everyone in their games plays with everything on.

I bought the unlock but it isn't showing

It usually appears within a minute. Open Settings in the app to refresh. On a new phone or after reinstalling, tap Restore purchases while signed in to the same Apple Account. Still stuck? Email us your Player ID.

I have an offer code

In the app, open Settings and tap Redeem an offer code. If the sheet doesn't appear, use Redeem in the App Store and come back to the app afterwards.

Can I get my data deleted?

Game photos and data delete themselves after each game. To remove your player ID and unlock record too, email us your Player ID.
